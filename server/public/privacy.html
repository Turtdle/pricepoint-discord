Privacy Policy

Effective 10 September 2026

PricePoint for Discord (the "Activity") is a small, free Discord Activity. This page explains what data it handles. The short version: it stores your Discord display name, avatar, and your guesses for the day, shows your scores to the people in your channel, and nothing is sold or shared.

1. What is collected

When you open the Activity, Discord's Embedded App SDK authorises the app with the identify scope. From that the Activity receives and stores:

  • Your Discord user ID, display name, and avatar image URL
  • The ID of the Discord server and channel the Activity was opened in
  • Your guesses and scores for each day's puzzle, and the time you last made a guess

The Activity does not receive or store your email address, messages, friends list, or any other Discord data. The short-lived access token issued by Discord is used only to confirm your identity with Discord's API and to create a session; it is not stored on disk.

2. How it is used

  • To identify you across the five rounds and after a refresh
  • To show your name, avatar and per-round scores (never your guesses or the answers) to other people in the same channel, on the same day

No advertising, analytics, profiling, or tracking of any kind.

3. Who can see it

Anyone in the same Discord channel who opens the Activity on the same day sees your name, avatar, whether you're currently online in the Activity, and your five round scores. Your actual guesses and the answers are shown only to you.

4. Retention

Data is kept on the server's local disk for the current puzzle day and is discarded when the service is redeployed or restarted; in practice this is days, not months. Presence ("online") information is held in memory only. Nothing is backed up or archived.

5. Third parties

  • Discord — provides your identity via OAuth2 and hosts the Activity in its client. Subject to Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Amazon Web Services — the server runs on AWS App Runner in the us-east-1 region (United States). AWS processes network traffic and logs as our hosting provider.
  • pricepoint.gg — the daily puzzle and item photos are fetched by our server, not by your browser, so pricepoint.gg does not receive your IP address or identity from this Activity.

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone else.

6. Cookies and local storage

The Activity sets no cookies and does not use browser storage. A session identifier lives only in page memory while the Activity is open.

7. Children

The Activity is not directed at children and relies on Discord's own age requirements. If you believe a child has used it, contact us and the data will be removed.

8. Your choices

You can stop using the Activity at any time. To have your stored name, avatar and scores deleted before they expire naturally, contact us with your Discord user ID and it will be removed promptly.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date above will change when it is.
